# F351 — two marks and a gap, all measured in points nobody chose

Done 2026-11-26. `lib/ui/controls.zm` (`padded_for_mark`, `mark_air`, `panel_widget`), two tests.

Two of the three things the health-record port reported in one line each on 2026-11-26. The third
was its own and is recorded in that repository.

## A mark was inset further than the words beside it

> combobox arrow can be moved a little bit to trailing side

`chevron` is drawn in a square the height of a line and **spans the middle half of it** — the path
runs from 0.25 to 0.75. So a quarter of the side is already empty on the trailing edge, and the
control's own padding was added on top of that: the arrow sat half again further in than the text
at the other end. Measured in the running window at the port's look: the label's ink began 14
points from the leading edge and the arrow's ink ended **17 points** from the trailing one.

`padded_for_mark(style, side)` is `padded_for` with the trailing edge tightened by `side *
mark_air`, and `mark_air` is the same 0.25 the path is drawn with — **named, because two numbers
here would be a mark that moved when somebody redrew the arrow**. The four controls that put a
chevron at a padded trailing edge use it: the combo box, the date field, the expander and the
toolbar's overflow.

**It moves the mark and nothing else**, because the mark is the last child of a row with a `flex`
before it. The label is at the leading edge and keeps the padding it had.

Now the two insets are equal, which is the assertion: `a_combo_boxs_mark_is_inset_like_its_words`
measures the text's ink from one edge and the mark's ink from the other and insists they match.

## A panel charged spacing for a child that draws nothing

> on listview there is an unnecessary small gap at the top

`panel_widget` builds a column of exactly two children: a region that exists to be spoken and
draws nothing, and the content. Its spacing was `style.space.s` — so **every gap it could ever
place was between an invisible node and the content**. In the port that was a bare six-point band
of window colour between a screen's header and the first row of its list, on every panel in the
application.

Now `0.0`. There is nothing for spacing to separate.

**The general shape of it is left alone and written down here instead.** A `Stack` counts spacing
per child rather than per child that is *there*, so any zero-sized node earns a gap like any
other. Changing that would be a rule about what "there" means — a `Flex` spacer measures nothing
on its own axis, and so does a part wrapping `Nothing` — and a stack cannot tell a node that
happens to measure nothing from one that is deliberately empty. The panel never wanted a gap in
the first place, which is the smaller and the truer fix.
