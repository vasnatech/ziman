# F352 — a segment gave up more padding than it had

Done 2026-11-26. `lib/ui/controls.zm` (`segment_air`, `segment_inside`, `toggle_group_widget`),
two tests.

Raised by the health-record port: "filter buttons group height should be same as all button". Its
filter strip stood **18 points** tall beside **26-point** buttons.

## Why

`segment_inside` makes a segment shallower than a button:

```ziman
trimmed(f.style.padding.top, f.style.space.s)
```

and `trimmed` floors at zero — rightly, since "a look whose padding is already smaller than its
spacing step would otherwise ask for a negative inset". But a floor is not a cap: in a look whose
control padding **is** `space.s`, and the port's is 6 against 6, "a step shallower" comes out as
**no vertical padding at all**. The segment collapses onto its icon and the strip collapses with
it, down to the 2 points of track padding either side.

**The number was chosen against a different pair of numbers.** The comment above it cites
segmented controls that are `px-3 py-1` where their buttons are `px-4 py-2` — a difference of half
the padding, not all of it. The screen this was reported from is, in the target's own markup, a
`p-0.5` track around `py-1` buttons: the segments there have exactly the **same** vertical padding
as a standalone button, and the pill is the taller of the two by the track.

## The fix

One number, `segment_air(style)` — `space.xs`, the scale's smallest real step. It is both what the
track pads with and what each segment gives up, so

    (line + padding) - air - air + air + air

is a button's height in any look, **by construction rather than by a coincidence of the
register's numbers**. A segmented switch sits in a row beside buttons, and one that fails to line
up with them is the first thing anybody sees.

The segment is still shallower than a button — the lit chip is smaller than the strip around it,
which is the distinction the old rule was reaching for. What it is not is shallower by an amount
that can exceed what it has.

## Watched fail

| break | what reddened |
|---|---|
| a segment gives up `space.s` again | `a_toggle_group_is_exactly_a_button_tall`, and the roomy-look one |
| the track pads with something other than what a segment gave up | both of them |

The second test is the one that matters: it builds a look with 9-point padding and 3-point `xs`
and asserts the same equality, so the cancellation is arithmetic rather than a property of the
default theme — which is exactly what the defect was.

## What this did not settle

**An application may still want a denser strip than the toolkit's button.** The health-record port
pins every icon control in a row to its own `icon_button_side`, which is 24 against the toolkit's
26 and deliberately so; its filter strip is now wrapped in that height the same way its icon
buttons are. That is an application's judgement about its own density and not something `lib/ui`
should take a view on — but it only works because the toolkit's own answer is a height and not an
accident.
